A B2B business that needed to look like a partner, not a shop.

Genesis Films doesn't sell to drivers. It sells to dealerships and collision centers across metro Atlanta, who add XPEL paint protection film, window tint and ceramic coatings to the vehicles they sell and repair.

A general manager deciding whether to put Genesis on their menu wants three things fast: proof of the work, how the program runs, and how it makes the store money. So the site had to speak to dealers, not retail shoppers.

And the business had to show up wherever those dealers look: a Google search, a Maps listing, a question to an AI assistant, or the card left on a service desk.

Every page, doing its job.

Program pages
One per program, each ending in wholesale pricing
Who we serve
Separate pages for dealerships and collision centers
Work gallery
Real installs, filterable by service
Business cards
New emblem on the front, a Google review QR on the back

Seven pieces, each with one job.

A dealer-first website. A page for every program, a page for each audience, and a partner program page from first call to first install. It lets a GM qualify Genesis before the first meeting.

SEO. Location-focused titles, structured data for the business, every service, breadcrumbs and FAQs, Search Console verified and the sitemap submitted. Genesis shows up for the searches Atlanta dealers actually make.

AEO for AI search. An llms.txt summary built from the site's own data, AI crawlers welcomed, and FAQ answers marked up to be quoted. When someone asks ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini who runs XPEL programs for Atlanta dealers, Genesis is easy to find and describe.

Google profile & reviews. The Google Business Profile optimized alongside the launch, plus a QR code on every card that opens the review form in one scan.

Partner inquiries. A form emailed straight to the owner, with spam filtering and a hidden bot trap, so no inquiry gets lost.

Brand & print. A new metallic GF emblem across the site, favicon and app icon, print-ready business cards and a matching email signature.

Hosting & handover. Fast hosting on Vercel, the domain moved without touching the company's Microsoft 365 email, and a guide for whoever maintains it next.

Every path a dealer takes now points to the same answer.

Each piece does one job: get Genesis found, get it trusted, or turn interest into a conversation. Together they mean that wherever a dealer first meets Genesis, whether that's a search, a map listing, an AI answer or a card on the service desk, the next step is obvious.

And because we work AI-native, the whole rebuild, the search and AI setup, the emblem and the print files shipped in two days.
