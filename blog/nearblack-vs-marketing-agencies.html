NearBlack vs Marketing Agencies: Same Result, Different Price Tag

CategoryComparison

A full-service marketing agency quoted you $35,000 and a four-month timeline. A specialized studio can deliver the same website in three days for $1,999. This is not a quality difference. It is a structural one. Here is what you are actually paying for with each, and how to decide which is right for you.

Quick verdict

Full-service agency: Right when you need brand strategy, copywriting, photography, and ongoing marketing all under one roof. NearBlack: Right when you need your existing site replaced with something excellent, fast, at a fraction of the cost. Same website quality. Different scope and price.

What you are actually paying for at an agency

A $30,000 to $60,000 agency website quote is not inflated. It reflects how agencies are structured. A typical engagement includes:

  • An account manager who runs the relationship and bills by the hour
  • A project manager who coordinates internally and bills by the hour
  • A senior strategist who runs discovery workshops
  • A UX designer who builds wireframes and prototypes
  • A visual designer who produces mockups in Figma
  • A developer who turns the designs into a live site
  • A QA team member who tests across browsers and devices
  • Office overhead, software, business development costs, and margin

Every person on that list is billing hours. A four-month timeline at a six-person team is a lot of hours. The agency model is not a scam. It is just expensive because of how it is structured.

What you are paying for at NearBlack

NearBlack is a lean studio that does one thing: refresh existing websites. No discovery workshops. No wireframe phase. No Figma mockup presentations that take three weeks to approve. We take your current site, understand what it should be doing and is not, and build the replacement.

The process is faster because it is more focused. We have built a lot of these. We know what works. The overhead that an agency carries by necessity, we simply do not have.

The overhead gap

We built NearBlack after paying an agency tens of thousands of dollars for a site we did not like, then rebuilding the whole thing ourselves in a week. The output was better and cost a fraction of the price. That gap is not because agencies are dishonest. It is because their structure requires it.

When you hire an agency, you are hiring a system. That system has account management, project management, handoffs between departments, approvals, revisions, and a lot of meetings. Some businesses need that system. Many do not.

What agencies are genuinely better at

We are not the right choice for everything. Agencies have real advantages when you need:

  • Brand strategy built from scratch, including positioning and messaging
  • Professional copywriting across all pages
  • Photography and video production
  • Ongoing SEO content, PPC management, or social media
  • Enterprise-level complexity with custom integrations and security requirements

If you need all of that bundled together with an account team managing it indefinitely, an agency is the right structure. NearBlack is not a retainer relationship. We build your site, hand it over, and you own it.

When NearBlack is the clear choice

If you have an existing site that is not performing and you know roughly what needs to change, NearBlack delivers a professional replacement in 72 hours for a one-time fee. No workshops, no mockups, no six-month timelines. You tell us what the business does and what the site should accomplish. We build it. You review it. You launch it.

For most businesses with an existing web presence that has simply fallen behind, this is everything they actually need.

Bottom line

Agencies charge what they charge because of how they are built. NearBlack charges what it charges for the same reason. If you need a great website fast and without the overhead, NearBlack is the answer. If you need a full marketing operation, budget for an agency.

Frequently asked questions

Why do marketing agencies charge so much for websites?
Agencies have significant overhead: account managers, project managers, strategists, multiple rounds of discovery and approval, and billing structures that charge for every hour from every team member. A studio that focuses on one specific deliverable has none of that overhead.
Is a NearBlack website refresh as good as an agency build?
For a website refresh, yes. NearBlack focuses exclusively on refreshing existing sites, which means the design quality, performance, and SEO setup are professional-grade. What you are not paying for is brand strategy, copywriting, and ongoing retainer that agencies bundle in.
When should I use a full-service agency instead of NearBlack?
Use an agency when you need brand strategy from scratch, comprehensive copywriting, photography, video, or an ongoing marketing retainer. NearBlack does one thing extremely well: taking an existing site and making it significantly better.
Can NearBlack work alongside an agency?
Yes. Some clients use NearBlack to get a fast, professional site live immediately, then work with an agency on longer-term strategy and content. The two are not mutually exclusive.
