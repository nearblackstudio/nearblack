Pricing questions

What you pay, when you pay it and what's included.

Are there monthly fees?

Packages are a one-time price. Growth includes 6 months of hosting, and Authority includes 12 months of hosting and 6 months of maintenance. After that, hosting is $49 a month, or $129 a month with a monthly maintenance session.

How does payment work?

A 10% deposit starts your project. You pay the balance only when your new site goes live.

Is SEO included in every package?

Yes. Every package includes the SEO setup: page titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and Google Business Profile optimization. Every site is also built to be read by AI assistants like ChatGPT and Gemini. Growth and Authority add advanced SEO.

Which package is right for my business?

Business suits most local service businesses that need a credible site of up to 6 pages. Growth is for businesses that want more leads and to rank for more searches, with up to 10 pages, a blog and a lead capture form. Authority is for established companies that need up to 20 pages, content migration, booking and a competitor analysis.
